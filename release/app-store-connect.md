## Description

GarageTreasure helps you discover local garage sales, yard sales, moving sales, and estate sales near you. Every listing is reviewed by our team before it goes live, so you can browse with more confidence and less noise.

Explore sales on the map, scan nearby listings, view sale details, and confirm source information before you go. GarageTreasure is built for people who love weekend finds, vintage pieces, antiques, tools, furniture, collectibles, and hidden local treasures.

Starting in the Murrieta and Temecula area, GarageTreasure focuses on curated local discovery instead of spammy, outdated listings. We review public sale information and user-submitted sale leads before they appear in the app.

Use GarageTreasure to:
- Find nearby sales on a map
- Browse reviewed local listings
- Check dates, times, cities, and categories
- Open source details for curated estate sale listings
- Submit a sale lead for review

GarageTreasure is currently focused on Southwest Riverside County, including Murrieta, Temecula, Wildomar, Menifee, Lake Elsinore, French Valley, and Canyon Lake.

No spam. No outdated listings. Just curated local sales near you.
