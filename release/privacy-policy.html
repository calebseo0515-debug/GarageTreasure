GarageTreasure Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

GarageTreasure helps people discover curated local garage sales, yard sales, moving sales, and estate sales near them. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information We Use

GarageTreasure may use your approximate location while you use the app to show nearby sales on the map. Your location is used for the app experience and is not sold.

If you choose to email a sale for review, your email app may include the information you decide to send, such as sale details, city, date, and contact information. GarageTreasure uses that information only to review the listing.

Listing Data

GarageTreasure displays curated local sale information. Some listings may link to the original public source so users can confirm exact addresses, times, and organizer details before visiting.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as maps, hosting, analytics, app distribution, email, and database providers. These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

GarageTreasure does not sell personal information. We may share information only when needed to operate the app, comply with law, protect safety, or review user-submitted sale information.

Children

GarageTreasure is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
